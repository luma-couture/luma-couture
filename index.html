The House

Made to be remembered.

Luma Couture is an independent fashion house built around one-of-a-kind design, dramatic structure and garments with a point of view. Each piece is approached as an object of identity—not simply clothing.

The Designer

Annie Byers

Founder and designer of Luma Couture, Annie Byers creates work at the intersection of gothic romanticism, architectural tailoring and couture craft. In 2026, Luma presented its debut collection, Dominion, in Milan.
